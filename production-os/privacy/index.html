Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Reimagining India Production OS uses information when authorized to interact with Google Drive.

Google Drive access

Reimagining India Production OS uses Google OAuth authorization and requests the drive.file permission for production-file workflows.

Google Drive data accessed through this authorization is used only to support the media-production workflow for Reimagining India.

How information is used

Authorized Google Drive information is used for functions related to production assets, including identifying, processing and managing files used by the Production OS.

Google user data is not sold and is not used for advertising.

Storage and security

OAuth credentials and production-system data are maintained within access-controlled production infrastructure. Access is limited to the operation and administration of the Production OS.

Sharing

Google user data accessed by the Production OS is not shared with third parties for advertising, marketing or data-broker purposes.

Revoking access

Google account authorization for the Production OS can be revoked through the security and third-party access settings of the associated Google Account.

Contact

Questions regarding this policy or the Production OS can be directed to reimaginingindia2026@gmail.com.
